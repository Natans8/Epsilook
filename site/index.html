How search works

Anatomy of a search

The bar draws what it understood. Learn its six marks and you can read any search at a glance — this one narrows the whole game down to a single spell.

  • model: Column tag searches one column only
  • caster Category word one of the kinds that column holds
  • attach chest Word and its value the one word after it
  • | Either one also a comma, between numbers
  • "fel reaver" Exact phrase unquoted, the words match separately
  • −mech: Exclude drop everything that matches

Tags all have to match, and a tag takes one word unless quotes hold more together. So where a word sits is the whole question:

  • one model that is both
  • two different models on one spell
  • a chain effect that is also red
  • a chain effect — and “red” anywhere at all
